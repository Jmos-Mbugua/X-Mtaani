# X-Mtaani

X-Mtaani is a localStorage-backed civic reporting MVP with an X/Twitter-style
feed for anonymous community issue reports.

## Trust & Safety

X-Mtaani reduces misuse in the MVP through:

- Anonymous posting with public location protection, so exact street/location
  details are hidden on public feed cards.
- Verification badges such as Unverified, Community Confirmed, and Resolved.
- Archive controls that let users hide their own posts from public views while
  preserving local audit history.
- Community confirmations through comments and future signal aggregation.
- A moderation roadmap for evidence review, rate limits, and trusted community
  verifiers.
- Election-period safeguards against manipulation, harassment, ethnic hate, and
  incitement.
